    <h3 class="book-section__heading">What's inside</h3>
    <div class="book-chapters">
      <div class="book-chapter">
        <span class="book-chapter__num">Prologue</span>

          <strong class="book-chapter__q">Why do zebras have stripes?</strong>
          <p class="book-chapter__desc">We model Turing patterns emerging from reaction-diffusion systems and see how complexity arises from simple molecular rules.</p>

      <div class="book-chapter">

        <div class="book-chapter__body">
          <strong class="book-chapter__q">How do cells regulate their own genes?</strong>
          <p class="book-chapter__desc">We explore transcription factor networks and the recurring structural motifs that evolution has discovered independently, again and again.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="book-chapter">
        <span class="book-chapter__num">Module 2</span>
        <div class="book-chapter__body">

          <p class="book-chapter__desc">We decode <em>E. coli</em>'s chemotaxis algorithm — a robust, adaptive exploration strategy built from a handful of proteins.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="book-chapter">

          <p class="book-chapter__desc">We compare coronavirus spike proteins using structural biology and molecular dynamics to understand viral infectivity at the atomic level.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
      <div class="book-chapter">
